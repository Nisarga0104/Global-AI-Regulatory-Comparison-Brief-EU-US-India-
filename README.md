## Core Analytical Takeaways

1. **EU Digital Omnibus Impact:** The Digital Omnibus on AI deferred substantive Annex III high-risk compliance to **2 December 2027**, but baseline transparency obligations (Article 50) remain enforceable as of August 2026.
2. **US State Instability:** US compliance must map to core risk controls rather than volatile individual state statutes, as demonstrated by Colorado repealing SB 24-205 and enacting SB 26-189 within 2026.
3. **India's Principle-Based Posture:** India prioritizes public trust through its 7 foundational "Sutras" (Trust, People First, Innovation over Restraint, Fairness & Equity, Accountability, Understandable by Design, Safety & Resilience), utilizing existing statutes (IT Act, DPDP Act, RBI Directions) rather than a standalone AI law.

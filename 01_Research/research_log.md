# Research Log — AI Regulatory Comparison Brief
Date: 2026-09-13

Dated log of what was checked. This brief was the highest fabrication/staleness risk of the five
projects given how many specific dates, regulation numbers, and case names it contains — verified
systematically rather than spot-checked.

## Search 1 — "EU AI Act Digital Omnibus deferral high-risk 2026 2027 regulation signed July 2026"
Purpose: verify Regulation (EU) 2026/1744's exact dates and deferral terms before trusting the
draft's very specific claims.
Result: **Fully confirmed, no correction needed.** Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on
AI) was signed 8 July 2026, published in the Official Journal 24 July 2026, entered into force
27 July 2026. Defers Annex III standalone high-risk obligations from 2 August 2026 to 2 December
2027, and Annex I embedded high-risk from 2 August 2027 to 2 August 2028. Article 50 transparency
obligations were NOT deferred (apply from 2 August 2026 as originally scheduled), except the
Article 50(2) watermarking sub-obligation specifically, pushed to 2 December 2026 via a separate
transitional provision. Every one of these figures in the original draft was independently
verified against multiple current legal-tracking sources and matches exactly.

## Search 2 — "Colorado AI Act SB 24-205 repealed May 2026 SB 26-189 xAI DOJ magistrate stay"
Purpose: verify the Colorado repeal-and-replace timeline and the DOJ intervention claim, since
this is an unusual, easily-garbled sequence of events.
Result: **Fully confirmed, no correction needed.** Federal magistrate judge stayed enforcement of
the original SB 24-205 on 27 April 2026, following a lawsuit by xAI in which the DOJ intervened
(the first federal intervention against a state AI law) — draft says "April 2026," which is
accurate (source specifies 27 April). Governor Polis signed SB 26-189 on 14 May 2026, repealing
SB 24-205 outright and replacing it with the narrower Automated Decision-Making Technology Act,
effective 1 January 2027. Colorado's AG has stated neither law will be enforced until rulemaking
concludes — confirmed as of source dates in July-August 2026.

## Search 3 — "Texas TRAIGA HB 149 effective January 2026 California SB 53 frontier AI signed 2025"
Purpose: verify Texas and California statute names, dates, and scope claims.
Result: **Fully confirmed, no correction needed.** TRAIGA (HB 149) signed by Governor Abbott 22
June 2025, effective 1 January 2026, conduct-based (prohibited uses) rather than a broad
high-risk regime, 60-day cure period, no private right of action -- all confirmed. California SB
53 (Transparency in Frontier AI Act) signed by Governor Newsom 29 September 2025, effective 1
January 2026, applies to large frontier-model developers (>10^26 FLOPS) -- confirmed, and the
draft's assessment that it "would not directly govern a fintech's credit-scoring model" is
consistent with its actual scope. One completeness note, not an error: several trackers describe
Texas as the *third* state to enact comprehensive AI law (after Colorado and Utah), and Utah's
SB 149 is not mentioned anywhere in the original draft's US section. This isn't a factual error --
the draft doesn't claim to be exhaustive -- but is worth knowing as a scope gap if this brief is
extended later.

## Search 4 — "India AI Governance Guidelines MeitY November 2025 sutras seven principles IndiaAI Mission"
Purpose: verify the publication date, the "sutras" framing, and the specific principle named as
anchoring the framework.
Result: **Publication date, institutional structure, and general framing are all confirmed.**
MeitY published the India AI Governance Guidelines on 5 November 2025 under the IndiaAI Mission;
the three proposed institutions (AI Governance Group, Technology & Policy Expert Committee, AI
Safety Institute) match the draft exactly.
**One real error found and corrected:** the draft names "Do No Harm" as the anchoring/first
principle among the seven sutras. This does not appear in any primary or secondary source found
for the actual framework. The real seven sutras, confirmed across MeitY's own published materials
and multiple independent legal trackers, are: **Trust** (the actual anchoring principle, positioned
first specifically because the framework treats public and institutional trust as the precondition
for everything else), People First, Innovation over Restraint, Fairness & Equity, Accountability,
Understandable by Design, and Safety, Resilience & Sustainability. "Do No Harm" was replaced with
the correct sutra list and the correct anchoring principle (Trust) in the final draft.

## Net changes applied
- India section (Section 5): "Do No Harm" corrected to "Trust" as the anchoring principle, and
  the full seven-sutra list corrected to match the verified real framework.
- Everything in the EU, US, and remaining India sections required no correction -- an unusually
  clean result for a document this dense with fast-moving, specific facts, and worth noting as
  evidence the original drafting process was already careful, not just lucky.
